# Domain Scope

Perfectπ is domain-neutral numerical infrastructure.

## Primary target domains

- aerospace, orbital mechanics, navigation, and deep-space systems;
- electrical/electronics engineering, AC systems, RF, electromagnetics, DSP, power electronics, motors, and controls;
- mechanical, civil, construction, manufacturing, machining, surveying, and CAD/CAM;
- quantum computing and quantum physics;
- particle, nuclear, and high-energy physics;
- biomedical engineering, imaging, biomechanics, instrumentation, and computational medicine;
- computer science, embedded systems, robotics, graphics, games, simulation, operating systems, and WebAssembly;
- general scientific and numerical computing;
- quantitative finance, probability, statistics, and Monte Carlo work.

## Scope boundary

Perfectπ owns π, derived constants where justified, representations, precision semantics, conversions, and verification.

It does not own:

- currencies or accounting arithmetic;
- unit systems;
- quantum gate frameworks;
- orbital mechanics models;
- medical algorithms;
- electrical circuit solvers;
- construction standards;
- general arbitrary-precision mathematics.

Those systems should be able to consume Perfectπ without Perfectπ duplicating their responsibilities.
