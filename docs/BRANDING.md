# Perfectπ Branding

## Official names

- Public brand: **Perfectπ**
- Plain-text name: **Perfect Pi**
- Cargo package: `perfect-pi`
- Rust crate/import: `perfect_pi`
- Repository: `perfect-pi`

## Brand principles

The visual identity communicates mathematical precision, engineering trust, efficiency, deterministic behavior, and broad scientific applicability without looking tied to Raspberry Pi hardware, food/pie imagery, cryptocurrency, or a single engineering field.

## Official logo

The official Perfectπ mark is a **geometric π inside a segmented bounded-precision ring**.

The central π makes the mathematical identity immediate. The surrounding ring represents bounded precision, deterministic limits, and fixed resource bounds. Its deliberate segmentation reflects explicit, opt-in capability rather than hidden runtime cost.

The mark is designed to remain recognizable at GitHub-avatar size and to work in monochrome.

## Visual meaning

- **π** — the project's numerical subject and unmistakable identity.
- **Bounded ring** — explicit precision limits and fixed resource bounds.
- **Segmented/open ring** — deliberate capability selection and opt-in cost.
- **Strong geometric silhouette** — deterministic engineering and small-size readability.

## Explicit exclusions

Perfectπ branding must not use or imply:

- Raspberry Pi hardware or its visual identity;
- literal pie or food imagery;
- cryptocurrency/coin motifs;
- a single engineering discipline;
- generic gears as the primary identity;
- decorative complexity that becomes unreadable at small sizes.

## Usage guidance

### GitHub avatar

Use `perfect-pi-icon-dark-512.png`.

Do not use the wordmark or tagline as a small avatar.

### README, documentation, and website headers

Use the icon or `perfect-pi-wordmark-dark.svg` depending on the available horizontal space.

### GitHub social preview

Use `perfect-pi-github-social-preview-1280x640.png`.

### Monochrome reproduction

Use `perfect-pi-icon-monochrome.svg`.

## Clear space and modification rules

- Preserve generous clear space around the mark.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the artwork.
- Do not arbitrarily recolor the official assets.
- Do not add gradients, glows, drop shadows, bevels, or ornamental effects to the official mark.
- Prefer the icon alone at small sizes.
- Preserve the π silhouette and bounded-ring relationship in any future approved derivative.

## Brand status

The initial official Perfectπ logo and brand assets are committed to the repository and are the current project identity.
