# Perfectπ Commercial Licensing

Commercial use of Perfectπ is encouraged, but it is not granted by the community license.

## When a commercial license is required

A separate written paid commercial license from DrTomLLC is required before using Perfectπ for a commercial purpose that is outside the permissions of the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. This includes production use in a commercial product or service, commercial redistribution, OEM or embedded distribution, paid hosted services, and other revenue-producing or commercially advantageous use.

## Commercial terms

Commercial licenses may use the structure appropriate to the deployment, including a negotiated license fee, royalty or revenue-share arrangement, per-product or per-deployment terms, or another written commercial arrangement.

There is intentionally no fixed public royalty percentage in this repository. Commercial consideration and scope are agreed in writing for the specific use.

A commercial agreement may address matters such as:

- internal production use;
- redistribution in proprietary products;
- SaaS or hosted-service use;
- OEM, embedded, firmware, or device distribution;
- sublicensing needed for a distribution chain;
- support, maintenance, indemnity, warranty, or service commitments when separately agreed.

No commercial right, support obligation, warranty, indemnity, or service-level commitment is implied until it is stated in a signed commercial agreement.

## Community use remains separate

The commercial path does not remove the community/noncommercial permissions in LICENSE. Users operating entirely within those permissions do not need a commercial agreement.

Commercial licensing inquiries should be directed to the project owner through the official DrTomLLC / Perfectπ repository contact channels.
